Remote Visio is now running in the menu bar.

  1. macOS may ask to allow System Audio Recording and Microphone access for Remote Visio. Allow both; without them the other side hears silence from this Mac.
  2. When macOS asks about the camera extension, approve it: System Settings > General > Login Items & Extensions > Camera Extensions. Remote Visio Camera then appears as a camera in video apps.
  3. In the app that needs the microphone (Zoom, dictation, anything), choose Remote Visio as the input device.
  4. Click the Remote Visio icon in the menu bar to copy the address, and open it in a browser on the device you will speak from. The first time, the browser warns about the self-signed certificate; continue past it.

To remove Remote Visio later, choose Uninstall Remote Visio… from its menu-bar menu, or run /Applications/RemoteVisio.app/Contents/Resources/uninstall.sh in Terminal.
