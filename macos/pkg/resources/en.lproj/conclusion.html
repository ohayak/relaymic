Remote Visio is now running in the menu bar.

  1. Click the Remote Visio icon in the menu bar, choose Install Browser Extension… and follow the steps it shows. The extension gives the web pages of Chromium browsers (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc …) three devices: Remote Visio Microphone, Remote Visio Speaker and Remote Visio Camera. Desktop apps do not see the microphone and speaker.
  2. When macOS asks about the camera extension, approve it: System Settings > General > Login Items & Extensions > Camera Extensions. Remote Visio Camera then appears as a camera in video apps.
  3. Click the Remote Visio icon in the menu bar to copy the address, and open it in a browser on the device you will speak from. The first time, the browser warns about the self-signed certificate; continue past it.
  4. On your meeting's website, choose Remote Visio Microphone, Remote Visio Speaker and Remote Visio Camera as the microphone, speaker and camera, and click Allow when asked.

To remove Remote Visio later, choose Uninstall Remote Visio… from its menu-bar menu, or run /Applications/RemoteVisio.app/Contents/Resources/uninstall.sh in Terminal.
