This policy explains what remotevisio.com stores in your browser, and how you choose. It covers this website only. The Remote Visio app and its browser extension use no cookies at all (see the end of this page).

## The short version

- Before you choose, the site sets no cookies and loads no analytics.
- If you click **Accept**, the site loads Google Analytics, which sets two cookies.
- If you click **Decline**, nothing else is stored apart from your choice itself.
- If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we treat it as **Decline** and do not show the banner. You can still accept in **Cookie settings**.
- You can change your mind at any time with **Cookie settings** at the bottom of every page.

## What is stored without your consent

Only what is strictly needed to remember your choices. It stays in your browser's local storage, is never sent to us or to anyone else, and is not a cookie:

- **Your cookie choice** (key `rv-consent`): whether you accepted or declined analytics, and the date. We keep it so the banner doesn't come back on every page. We ask you again after 6 months.
- **Your light or dark theme** (key `theme`), only if you use the theme switch.

## What is stored only if you accept

If you click Accept, the site loads Google Analytics 4 (property G-RMG8MNGGZQ), a service of Google. Google Analytics sets these first-party cookies on remotevisio.com:

- **`_ga`**: distinguishes one visitor from another with a random identifier, so we can count visitors rather than page views. It expires after 2 years.
- **`_ga_RMG8MNGGZQ`**: keeps track of the current visit (session) for this site. It expires after 2 years.

Each new visit can renew these lifetimes. We use the resulting statistics only to understand which pages are useful and how visitors find the site. We do not use them for advertising and do not combine them with other data to identify you. Our [Privacy Policy](/privacy) explains what Google receives and where it is processed, and Google describes [how it uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Changing your choice

Click **Cookie settings** at the bottom of any page to open the banner again, then choose Accept or Decline. If you withdraw your consent, we stop loading Google Analytics and delete its `_ga` and `_ga_RMG8MNGGZQ` cookies from your browser. Withdrawing does not affect what was collected before.

You can also delete cookies and site data in your browser's settings, or block them altogether. The site works the same without them. Google also offers a [browser add-on to opt out of Google Analytics](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout) on all websites.

## No cookies in the app or the extension

- The **Remote Visio Mac app** sets no cookies.
- The **sending web page** served by your Mac sets no cookies. It saves your microphone, camera and speaker choices in your browser's local storage, on your sending device only.
- The **browser extension** "Remote Visio Camera" sets and reads no cookies. It saves its settings and your answers for each site in the browser's extension storage, on your computer only.

None of these send anything to us. Our [Privacy Policy](/privacy) lists exactly what each one stores and how to delete it.
